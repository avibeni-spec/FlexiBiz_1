# FlexiBiz - Product Requirements Document (PRD) ## 1. Project Overview **FlexiBiz** is a premium management platform designed for small businesses in the beauty and hair salon industry. It aims to provide a seamless, elegant, and professional experience for business owners to manage their clients, appointments, and business settings. ## 2. Target Audience Business owners and managers in the beauty, spa, and hair salon sectors who value a modern, clean, and high-end aesthetic. ## 3. Core Features (Manager App) ### 3.1 Dashboard (4 Main Screens) * **Home:** Overview of today's appointments, key metrics (total revenue/appointments), and quick actions. * **Clients:** Searchable list of all clients with quick contact options. * **Calendar (Appointments):** Daily/Weekly view of scheduled services with status indicators. * **Settings:** Business profile, operating hours, and app preferences. ### 3.2 Sub-Screens & Workflows * **Client Management:** Add new client, edit existing client details, view appointment history and notes. * **Appointment Management:** Schedule new appointments, edit existing ones, update status (Booked, Completed, Canceled). * **Business Profile:** Manage business name, category, and owner details. ## 4. Technical Architecture * **Frontend:** Flutter (Modular, clean code). * **Backend:** Firebase Firestore (Real-time data synchronization). * **State Management:** Riverpod or Provider (Recommended for scalability and testability). * **Data Models:** * **Client:** `id`, `fullName`, `phone`, `notes`, `appointmentIds[]`. * **Appointment:** `id`, `dateTime`, `serviceType`, `clientId`, `status`. * **Business:** `id`, `name`, `type`, `operatingHours`, `ownerDetails`. ## 5. Design Principles (Luxurious Dark Mode) * **Theme:** Deep dark backgrounds (e.g., #121212 or deep navy). * **Typography:** Thin, elegant white text. * **Components:** Rounded white buttons, clean cards, minimal clutter. * **Navigation:** Intuitive bottom navigation bar. ## 6. Implementation Roadmap 1. **Phase 1:** Design System & Theme setup. 2. **Phase 2:** Core UI Screens (Home, Clients, Calendar, Settings). 3. **Phase 3:** CRUD Operations (Clients & Appointments) with Firestore integration. 4. **Phase 4:** Advanced Features (Notifications, Analytics). 5. **Phase 5:** Optimization & Store Readiness.
