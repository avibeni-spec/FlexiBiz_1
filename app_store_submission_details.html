# FlexiBiz - App Store & Google Play Submission Guide This document outlines the detailed requirements and steps to submit **FlexiBiz** (Manager and Client apps) to the Apple App Store and Google Play Store. ## 1. Marketing Assets (The "Storefront") Your app's first impression is critical. Prepare these assets for both stores: ### A. App Icons * **iOS:** 1024x1024 px (No transparency). * **Android:** 512x512 px + Feature Graphic (1024x500 px). ### B. Screenshots (High-Fidelity) * **Required Sizes (iOS):** 6.5" (iPhone 15/14/13/12 Pro Max) and 5.5" (iPhone 8 Plus). * **Required Sizes (Android):** Phone, 7-inch tablet, and 10-inch tablet. * **Pro Tip:** Use framed screenshots with short, punchy benefit-driven captions (e.g., "Manage your salon in seconds", "Luxury booking for your clients"). ### C. App Metadata * **Name:** FlexiBiz (or your branded name). * **Subtitle (iOS):** Premium Salon Management. * **Description:** Highlight core features (Real-time sync, Client management, Analytics). * **Keywords (iOS):** salon, booking, management, beauty, spa, appointments. * **Support & Privacy URLs:** You must have a basic website with a Support link and a Privacy Policy. --- ## 2. Technical Requirements ### A. iOS (App Store Connect) 1. **Bundle ID:** Must match the one in your Firebase/Xcode project. 2. **App Record:** Create a new app in [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/). 3. **Certificates & Profiles:** Generate a **Distribution Certificate** and a **Provisioning Profile** in the Apple Developer Portal. 4. **Privacy Info:** Apple requires you to disclose what data you collect (Contact info, Identifiers). 5. **Review Note:** Provide a test account (Email/Password) so the reviewer can log in and see the dashboard. ### B. Android (Google Play Console) 1. **Package Name:** Must match `applicationId` in `build.gradle`. 2. **App Bundle (AAB):** Use the command `flutter build appbundle --release`. 3. **App Content:** You must complete the "App Content" section (Ads, Age Rating, Privacy Policy, Data Safety). 4. **Testing Tracks:** Start with an **Internal Testing** track to verify the build before going to Production. --- ## 3. Legal & Privacy Both stores require a **Privacy Policy**. Since FlexiBiz handles personal client data, ensure your policy covers: * Data storage (Firebase). * Encryption and Security. * User rights to delete their account/data. --- ## 4. Final Checklist before "Submit for Review" - [ ] **Release Obfuscation:** Ensure you used `--obfuscate` in your build command to protect your logic. - [ ] **Firebase Rules:** Ensure your security rules are set to `Production` mode (not test mode). - [ ] **Broken Links:** Check that all buttons and navigation flows work perfectly on a real device. - [ ] **Permissions:** Ensure your `Info.plist` and `AndroidManifest.xml` have clear descriptions for why you need Camera/Notification access. **FlexiBiz is built to impress. Once your metadata is ready, hit that Submit button!**
