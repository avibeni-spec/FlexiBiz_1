# App Store Connect Setup Guide for FlexiBiz This guide will walk you through the essential steps to set up your app in App Store Connect, ensuring a smooth submission process for your luxury hair salon platform. ## 1. Prerequisites Before you begin, ensure you have: * An active **Apple Developer Program** membership. * Your **Bundle ID** (e.g., `com.yourname.flexibiz`) registered in the Apple Developer Portal. * A finished **Release Build** (IPA file) uploaded via Xcode or Transporter. --- ## 2. Create Your App Record 1. Log in to [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/). 2. Go to **My Apps** and click the **"+"** button > **New App**. 3. **Platforms:** Select iOS. 4. **Name:** "FlexiBiz: Luxury Salon Manager" (or your unique name). 5. **Primary Language:** English (US). 6. **Bundle ID:** Select the ID you created for FlexiBiz. 7. **SKU:** A unique ID for your internal tracking (e.g., `flexibiz_prod_01`). 8. **User Access:** Full Access. --- ## 3. App Information & Pricing * **App Information:** Under the "General" tab, set your **Privacy Policy URL**. This is mandatory. * **Pricing and Availability:** * Set your price (Free or Paid). * Select the countries where the app will be available. * **Tip:** For luxury salon software, "Free" with "In-App Purchases" is a common model if you plan to charge for premium features later. --- ## 4. Version Setup (The "Storefront") This is where you enter the details your clients will see. ### A. Screenshots * Upload the **High-Fidelity Assets** I prepared earlier for 6.5" and 5.5" displays. * Ensure the first 3 screenshots highlight your best features (Dashboard, Calendar, Booking). ### B. Descriptions & Keywords * **Description:** Use the "Hair Salon Edition" copy from our marketing guide. * **Keywords:** Use the SEO list we generated (salon, booking, stylist, etc.). * **Support URL:** A simple website or even a Facebook page with contact info. ### C. Build Selection * Once your build has finished processing (after uploading from Xcode), click the **"+"** next to "Build" and select your version. --- ## 5. App Privacy (Nutrition Labels) Apple is very strict here. Use the **Firebase Privacy Guide** [Text] I provided to answer the questions: * **Data Collection:** Disclose that you collect Contact Info, Identifiers, and Usage Data. * **Tracking:** Answer **No**, as we use data only for app functionality and analytics. --- ## 6. App Review Information The Apple reviewer needs to be able to log in to see the app's functionality. * **Sign-in required:** Check this box. * **User name/Password:** Provide a "Manager" test account (create one in your Firestore/Auth console first). * **Notes:** "This is a management suite for hair salons. Use the provided credentials to access the manager dashboard. The client booking flow can be accessed via the home screen." --- ## 7. Submission 1. Click **Save**. 2. Click **Add for Review**. 3. Answer the final export compliance and advertising questions. 4. Click **Submit to App Review**. **Review Time:** Typically takes 24–48 hours. Keep an eye on your email for any "Resolution Center" messages from Apple. **FlexiBiz is ready to shine!** Let me know if you hit any snags during the process.
