# FlexiBiz - Product Requirements Document (PRD) - Refined for Hair Salons ## 1. Project Overview **FlexiBiz** is a premium management platform designed for the luxury hair salon and beauty industry. It provides a seamless, high-end ecosystem for salon owners to manage artists, chairs, and clients with editorial precision. ## 2. Target Audience High-end hair salons, independent stylists, and beauty studios that prioritize brand aesthetics and professional guest management. ## 3. Core Features (Manager App) ### 3.1 Dashboard (4 Main Screens) * **Home (The Pulse):** Real-time metrics for daily chair turnover, stylist revenue, and upcoming appointments. * **Clients (The Inner Circle):** Detailed guest profiles including **Color History Logs** and treatment preferences. * **Calendar (The Studio Floor):** High-fidelity view of all chairs and stylists with multi-hour session support (e.g., Balayage, Treatments). * **Settings:** Business profile, operating hours, and staff management. ### 3.2 Sub-Screens & Workflows * **Guest Management:** Add/Edit clients with specific fields for hair type, skin sensitivity, and permanent color formulas. * **Artisan Scheduling:** Precision booking for complex services, with status tracking (Checked-in, Processing, Finished). * **Business Profile:** Manage salon branding, location, and social media links. * **Aura Analytics:** Executive reports on stylist productivity and retail growth, with PDF export capability. ## 4. Technical Architecture * **Frontend:** Flutter (Modular, Feature-First approach). * **Backend:** Firebase Firestore (Real-time cloud synchronization). * **State Management:** Riverpod (Global state for loading, auth, and data streams). * **Security:** Production-ready Firestore rules with multi-tenant isolation. ## 5. Design Principles (Onyx & Alabaster) * **Creative North Star:** "The Digital Concierge." * **Theme:** Deep Onyx backgrounds, Alabaster thin typography, and minimal gold accents. * **Components:** Pill-shaped buttons, glassmorphic overlays, and subtle radial glows. ## 6. Implementation Roadmap 1. **Phase 1:** Design System & Theme setup (Completed). 2. **Phase 2:** Core Manager & Client UI (Completed). 3. **Phase 3:** Real-time Firestore Integration (Completed). 4. **Phase 4:** Global Loading & UX Refinement (Completed). 5. **Phase 5:** Aura Analytics & PDF Export (Completed). 6. **Phase 6:** Niche-Specific Refinement (Hair Salon Focus - Current). 7. **Phase 7:** Store Deployment & Launch.
